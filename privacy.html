Piano Spirits

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Piano Spirits ("the App") is a piano app for playing, recording and learning, developed by PHAM VAN DAT. Your privacy matters deeply to us. This policy explains clearly what data the App handles and how.

1. Your Data

All recordings, photos you put on the keys, learning progress, settings, and preferences you create in the App are stored exclusively on your device. We have zero access to your content.

  • Piano recordings (video & audio)
  • Photos you choose for your keyboard (see section 3)
  • Learning progress (completed lessons, stars, streak, song practice history)
  • Your choices in the App, such as your light effect and keyboard
  • App settings and preferences

2. Audio

The App does not use your microphone. When you record a video, the App captures the piano sound it plays itself, together with the stage. Recordings are stored on your device only and are never uploaded or transmitted to any server.

3. Photos

Saving videos: if you choose "Save to Photos", the App asks for permission to add videos to your Photo Library. It can only add videos; it cannot read your library.

Your photo on the keys: if you add your own photo as a keyboard picture, you pick it with Apple's system photo picker, which gives the App only the photo you select (no access to the rest of your library). The App keeps a copy of that photo on your device to draw it on the keys. It is never uploaded, shared or analysed, and you can delete it anytime in the Keyboard screen.

4. Screen Recording

The App captures the piano stage view during recording sessions. Recordings are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted externally.

5. Subscriptions & Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app subscriptions processed entirely through Apple's App Store via StoreKit. We do not collect or store your payment information — all transactions are handled by Apple.

Some subscriptions may include a free trial. When the trial ends, the subscription renews automatically at the price shown before you start it, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in your Apple ID account settings. You are not charged during the trial.

To verify your subscription status and unlock Pro features, the App receives transaction information from Apple StoreKit (such as product ID, purchase date, and subscription status). Purchase events (the plan, its price and currency, whether a purchase was started, completed or cancelled) are also recorded in our analytics (section 6) and reported to the ad networks we use to measure our own campaigns (sections 7.2 and 7.5). We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. For details, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Analytics & Crash Reporting

The App uses Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config (by Google) to collect usage statistics and crash diagnostics, and to adjust app settings remotely (for example which subscription plans the Pro screen shows). Firebase identifies an installation with a random app instance identifier, not with your name or contact details. This helps us understand how the App is used and fix problems faster.

  • Screen views and in-app interactions (e.g. button taps, songs and lessons played, recordings started, saved or shared, ads watched, language changed)
  • Your choices in the App, such as the light effect and the keyboard picture you use (the name of a built-in picture, or simply "your photo" — never the photo itself)
  • Subscription screen views and purchase events, with the plan and its price
  • Crash reports with device model, OS version, and stack traces
  • No name, email address or other contact information is collected

Data handling is governed by Google's Firebase Privacy & Security.

7. Advertising

The App displays ads via Google AdMob for users on the free tier. Pro subscribers do not see any ads.

To serve ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising Identifier (IDFA) — only if you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • Coarse location (derived from IP address)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

If you decline tracking via the ATT prompt, AdMob will serve non-personalized ads instead. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Data shared with Google may be used for ad personalization and frequency capping. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and AdMob & AdSense Privacy.

7.1 AdMob Mediation Partners

To improve ad fill rate and revenue, AdMob mediates with third-party advertising networks. When mediation selects one of these networks to fill an ad request, the network's SDK (bundled in the App) may collect data — typically a hashed Advertising Identifier, coarse location, device information, and ad-interaction data — to serve ads. Each network operates under its own privacy policy:

  • AppLovin — https://www.applovin.com/privacy/
  • Meta Audience Network — https://www.facebook.com/about/privacy/
  • Unity Ads — https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) — https://vungle.com/privacy/
  • Pangle — https://www.pangleglobal.com/privacy
  • ironSource — https://www.is.com/legal/privacy-policy/
  • Mintegral — https://www.mintegral.com/en/privacy/
  • Chartboost — https://answers.chartboost.com/en-us/articles/200780269
  • myTarget — https://target.my.com/help/advertisers/privacypolicy/en

If you decline tracking via the ATT prompt or revoke consent in Settings → Ad Preferences, mediation partners will be limited to non-personalized ads only.

7.2 TikTok Ads Attribution

The App includes the TikTok Business SDK for the sole purpose of attributing installs and in-app subscription events to TikTok Ads campaigns. The SDK transmits limited app-event signals (app launch, install, subscribe) and a hashed Advertising Identifier (only if you grant ATT permission) to TikTok. The App does not display TikTok ads or upload personal content to TikTok.

For details, see TikTok's Privacy Policy and the TikTok Business Products (Data) Terms.

7.3 Apple Search Ads Attribution

The App uses Apple's AdServices framework to learn whether it was installed from an Apple Search Ads campaign. Apple returns only campaign-level information (such as campaign and keyword identifiers); no personal data is shared with us. For details, see Apple Search Ads & Privacy.

7.4 Notifications

If you turn on the daily practice reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. If a subscription with a free trial is offered and you start one, the App may also offer a reminder a day before the trial ends. These notifications are created and delivered entirely on your device; no push service or server is involved. You can turn them off anytime in the App's Settings or in iOS Settings.

7.5 Google Ads Conversion Measurement

The App includes Google's on-device conversion measurement, used together with Firebase Analytics to tell us whether our own Google Ads campaigns lead to installs and purchases. The measurement runs on your device; the App does not give Google any name, email address or phone number.

7.6 MIDI & Bluetooth

If you connect a MIDI keyboard over USB or Bluetooth, the App reads the notes you play to light up the stage. Bluetooth is used only to pair with your keyboard; nothing about the device or your playing leaves your iPhone or iPad.

8. GDPR Consent (EU/UK/Switzerland)

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App displays a consent dialog (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform) on first launch, asking for your consent to use third-party advertising services.

You can update or withdraw your consent at any time via Settings → Ad Preferences inside the App.

9. Data Deletion

You can delete any recording, and any photo you added to the keys, at any time from within the App. Learning progress and your choices are kept on your device only. Deleting the App from your device permanently removes all locally stored data.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and it is not marketed to them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
